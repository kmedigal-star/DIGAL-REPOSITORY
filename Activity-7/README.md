## 🎯 A. Objective of the Website

* To showcase the business's products and services professionally.
* To make it easier for consumers to know the latest updates, prices, and location of the Dessert Bakery.
* To inform consumers of the bakery's store hours and pickup/delivery times.
* To introduce consumers to the team behind the pastries.

---

## 🧠 B. Prompt Engineering & Website Features

Using Gemini, a detailed Markdown prompt was generated for Replit Agent covering:
* **Design & Aesthetic Guidelines:** Warm, rich color palette (deep pistachio green `#2D5A27`, warm chocolate/cream tones), elegant serif headings (*Playfair Display* / *Cormorant Garamond*), and a modern luxury bakery feel.
* **Core Structure & Pages/Sections:**
  1. **Header & Navigation:** Sticky navbar with logo, navigation links (*The menu*, *Our little story*, *Find us*), and dynamic shopping bag/cart counter.
  2. **Hero Section:** Headline (*"Life is short. Have the dessert."*), dual CTAs, and featured signature dessert (*Dubai Pistachio Cloud*).
  3. **Featured Signature Categories:** Filterable product grid across **Kataifi Puddings**, **Gourmet Pancakes**, and **Artisanal Pastries** (*Dubai Pistachio Cloud*, *Berry Velvet Pancakes*, *Citrus Silk Tart*, *Midnight Kunafa Cup*, *Peachy Keen Pancakes*, *Rosewater Morning Bun*) with interactive *Add to bag* buttons and product modals.
  4. **Customer Reviews & Social Proof:** Testimonial cards and Instagram highlight (*"Don't just take our crumbs for it"*).
  5. **Location, Catering & Newsletter:** Dubai-area map placeholder, catering inquiry form (*"Make room for one more"*), and email subscription footer.
